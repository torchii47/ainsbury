About

Free revision guides and study tools for computing students, by Rowan Ainsbury.

Who makes this site

I'm a T Level Digital specialist who has previously taught GCSE Computer Science. I started this site for my own students, and it's now open to any student or teacher who finds it useful.

What's here

  • T Level Digital Software Development core guide, for the Pearson specification.
  • GCSE Computer Science guide, for OCR J277.
  • Practice tools, demos and a student toolkit.
  • A page for teachers on using it all with a class.

How the guides are kept accurate

  • Each guide is written to its exam board's specification. Guide pages show when they were last reviewed.
  • Every page has a report a problem link, and so does every question.
  • Corrections go live for everyone as soon as they're made, with no new edition to wait for.

Mistakes can still happen, so always check important details against your exam board's specification.

Independent and free

This site isn't produced or endorsed by Pearson, OCR, JCQ or any other exam board. It's free to use, with no adverts, accounts or sign-ups. Your answers stay in your own browser. See the privacy page and terms of use.

Spotted a problem?

To report a mistake or ask about the site, use the report a problem page. Please don't include your name or any other personal details.
