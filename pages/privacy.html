Privacy

You don't need an account to use this site, and I can't see who you are or how you're doing. Last updated 6 November 2026.

The short version: your answers, marks and notes stay in your own browser. I count visits and a few actions, but not who did them. There are no cookies, no adverts and no sign-ups.

Who runs this site

This site is run by Rowan Ainsbury. To ask a question about your privacy, use the report a problem page.

What's saved on your device

The site saves your work in your browser's local storage, so it's still there when you come back. This includes:

  • your checklist ratings, answers, marks, quiz scores and flashcard progress
  • your notes
  • your name, if you type it into the progress summary box
  • your placement tracker entries, if you use it
  • your code, notes and timers from the practice tools
  • small settings, such as which course you picked on the homepage

Your browser also saves a copy of the site's pages so they work offline. That copy contains only the site itself, not your work.

None of your work is sent to me or to anyone else. If you make a progress file, a summary or a backup, it's up to you where it goes. On a shared computer, anyone using the same browser login may be able to see your work. To delete it, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

What's counted

I use GoatCounter to see which pages are useful. It uses no cookies and records:

  • which pages are visited, and the site a visitor came from
  • general details such as browser type, screen size and country
  • links clicked to other websites
  • some actions in the revision guides, such as "finished mock" or "searched"

These are totals. Your name, answers, scores and search words are never sent. GoatCounter uses your IP address and browser details for up to 8 hours to tell one visit from the next, but doesn't save them to its database or disk.

Other services this site uses

Like any website, these services receive your IP address when your browser connects to them:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site.
  • Google Fonts supplies the retro fonts.
  • GoatCounter supplies its counting script.
  • Google Forms collects reports, if you choose to send one.
  • jsDelivr supplies Python when you press ▶ Run on a code example.
  • The CargoPulse demo also loads tools from Tailwind, cdnjs and jsDelivr.

If you report a problem

Your report only reaches me if you send it. Reports are collected with Google Forms, so Google handles them too. Please don't include your name, email address, school or anything else that identifies you. If you do, I'll only use it to deal with your report.

Your rights

I don't keep accounts or personal records for visitors, so I usually can't link any data to you. You can still ask me about your data using the report a problem page. If you're worried about how your data is used, talk to a parent, carer or teacher, or contact the Information Commissioner's Office.
