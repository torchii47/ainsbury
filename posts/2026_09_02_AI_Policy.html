AI Policy

Date: 1st September 2026

IMPORTANT

At the core, it's believed that an online portfolio should serve as a genuine reflection of an author's individual capabilities, technical problem-solving skills, and personal craftsmanship. While artificial intelligence tools are becoming increasingly prevalent in software development, this platform strictly discourages the reliance on AI-generated code, design, or written content for portfolio projects.

Building projects manually from the ground up is essential for deep learning, solidifying foundational programming concepts, and building genuine engineering intuition. Prospective employers and collaborators review portfolios to evaluate your specific skills, code quality, and ability to reason through complex challenges—attributes that AI shortcuts obscure rather than highlight.

To maintain transparency and professional integrity, all code, written articles, and design assets showcased on this site are required to be human-authored. Demonstrating authentic skills, personal logic, and hands-on execution remains the most effective and respected way to advance your technical career.
