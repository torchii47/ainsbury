About this algorithm

Bubble sort ★ On the spec

■ BIG O IS FOR INTEREST ONLY

Big O notation isn't on the T Level spec. In the exam, compare algorithms by reasoning about how many comparisons they make, how long they take and how much memory they need. See 2.11 Common algorithms.

How to use it

Only five algorithms are on the T Level spec, and they're marked ★ On the spec:

  • Searching: linear search and binary search.
  • Sorting: bubble sort, insertion sort and merge sort.

Press Show non-specification algorithms to see quick sort, heap sort and the rest. They're good for seeing why some algorithms are faster than others, but you won't be asked about them.

  1. Choose Sorting or Searching, then an algorithm, a list size and a list type.
  2. Automatic plays it at the speed you set. Step goes one comparison at a time, so you can predict what happens next.
  3. Watch the Python code: the highlighted line is the one running.
  4. Compare the Counters for different algorithms on the same list size. That's the reasoning the exam wants.

Keyboard
