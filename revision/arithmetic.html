Order of operations

  1. Brackets
  2. ** (and two in a row work right to left: 2 ** 3 ** 2 is 2 ** 9)
  3. *, /, // and %, left to right
  4. + and -, left to right

✗ COMMON MISTAKE

Once a float is involved, the answer is a float. 6 / 2 prints 3.0, not 3, and 3.0 + 1 prints 4.0.

▲ STRETCH

// always rounds down, so -7 // 2 is -4, not -3. % matches it: -7 % 2 is 1, because -7 = 2 × -4 + 1.
